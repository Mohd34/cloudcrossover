Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Complete Client-Side Processing

CloudCrossover is designed with absolute privacy by default. All mathematical modeling, cost calculations, and ADR generation run 100% locally in your web browser. No workload parameters, traffic metrics, or company infrastructure details are ever transmitted to any remote server or third party.

2. No Cookies and No Tracking

CloudCrossover uses zero third-party tracking scripts, zero advertising trackers, and zero marketing pixels. Your user experience is completely free from intrusive telemetry.

3. Local Storage & URL Hashes

Your dark/light theme preference is stored locally on your device via standard HTML5 localStorage. Shared scenarios are encoded purely in client-side URL fragment hashes (e.g. #r=10000000...), which are not transmitted over HTTP request headers to hosting servers.

4. Open Source Transparency

The entire source code of this tool is open-source and auditable on GitHub at github.com/Mohd34/cloudcrossover.
